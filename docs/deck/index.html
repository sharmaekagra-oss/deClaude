skill/SKILL.md

The rulebook

  1. No em dashes. Ever.
  2. Add no facts.
  3. Keep every fact, list item and lesson.
  4. Keep the genre.
  5. Don't swap one tell for another.
  6. Cut more than you add.
  7. Keep the writer's voice and spelling.
  8. Every sentence with content needs a counterpart.

Takeaways

What we learned

A clean score can hide a bad edit

Early runs scored near zero while dropping a deadline's reason and a product's care instructions. So the app now checks facts, along with tells.

Fast models cut corners

Groq answers in seconds but dropped facts until we added sentence checks. One model stopped mid-sentence; we test for that too.

Free tiers are fragile

Gemini allows 20 requests a day per model, Groq 8,000 tokens a minute. A chain of providers keeps the app up.

Limits

Detectors tuned for English · formatting isn't kept on upload · a sentence full of tells can be cut whole, even when it held a real thank-you.

Built with AI

Claude Code (Opus 5.5) for research, code and tests · Groq, Gemini and OpenRouter free tiers run the rewrites · full log in docs/ai-log.md.
